The Problem

A Taste of Part III

The Math, Without the Mystery

Every formula comes with round numbers you can check in your head, and the mistake that most often breaks it.

Chapter 8

The "3x rule" isn't a rule

Required Coverage = 1 ÷ Win Rate

3x coverage is only right if you win about 33% of deals. A 25% win rate needs 4x, and an enterprise team at 15% needs closer to 7x.

Chapter 9

CAC that tells the truth

CAC = Total Sales & Marketing Cost ÷ New Customers

Leave out salaries and tools, and a real $1,040 CAC gets reported as $720. That number looks 30% better than reality.

Chapter 10

Retention is the real lever

LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin) ÷ Churn Rate

Cutting monthly churn from 5% to 4% lifts LTV 25% without spending one more dollar on acquisition.

Chapter 11

Never read NRR alone

105% NRR + 90% GRR = a masking problem

Strong expansion can hide a 10% revenue leak underneath. If the upsell motion slows, the churn problem shows up with nothing left to cover it.
