I Made It: Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026 (draft for attorney review)

This policy explains what Streak Business Solutions LLC ("Streak," "we," "us") collects for I Made It, why, who helps us run it, and the choices you have. It covers the waitlist and sales pages at streakbizsolutions.com/imadeit and the builder app at app.streakbizsolutions.com.

The short version: we collect what we need to build your site and nothing more. We don't sell your information, we don't share it for advertising, we don't use ad trackers or analytics cookies, and the AI we use may not train on your content. You can delete your account and data yourself at any time.

1. What we collect

When you join the waitlist

  • Your email address.
  • How you found us: the campaign tag on the link you used (for example, which LinkedIn post), or the page that referred you.
  • Your browser type (the "user agent") and the time you signed up.

When you create an account and build your site

  • Your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time link.
  • What you enter in the interview: your goals, the résumé and LinkedIn text you add, your answers about your work, praise you paste in, your privacy choices, and your contact details for the site (name, email, LinkedIn link, city).
  • If you upload a résumé or LinkedIn PDF, the file is read in your browser and only the text is sent to us. The file itself isn't uploaded.
  • Your headshot, if you add one. It's stored with your build so it can appear on your site. It is never sent to the AI.
  • The AI's drafts, the versions you approve, and your edits.
  • Beta feedback, if you choose to fill in the feedback form.

When you buy (once checkout opens)

  • Your email, what you bought, the amount, and the date. Our payment provider (Stripe) collects your card details; we never see or store your full card number.

Usage and technical records

  • A log of each AI run: the step, the kind of run, when it happened, how much AI text it used, and whether it worked. We use this to enforce fair-use limits and keep costs in check.
  • Our hosting and database providers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser, and request times) for security and to keep the service running.

When you email us

  • Your message and email address, so we can help.

What we don't collect: we don't ask for your Social Security number, date of birth, or home address, and we ask you not to include them. We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics.

2. How we use it

  • To run I Made It: sign you in, save your progress, generate drafts, show previews, and build your site files.
  • To keep it fair and safe: enforce usage limits, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
  • To email you about I Made It: waitlist updates, beta invitations, launch news, sign-in links, purchase confirmations, Toolbox reminders, and replies to your questions. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To improve I Made It: we look at usage records and the feedback you choose to send.
  • To follow the law: tax, accounting, and legal requests.

We don't sell your personal information. We don't share it for targeted advertising. We don't use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

3. The AI

To draft your content, we send the text needed for each step to our AI provider, Anthropic. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, it may not use your content to train its models, and it keeps API data only for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. Your headshot photo is never sent. Before any step runs, a quick automated check makes sure the request is about your site; that check also runs through Anthropic.

5. Where your information is kept

We operate in the United States, and our database is in the United States (Supabase, US East region). Some providers may process data in other locations under their own safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

Information How long

Your published site isn't stored by us. It lives on your own GitHub account, and deleting your I Made It account doesn't affect it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Download your site at any time from the Publish step.
  • Delete your account and data at any time: Account menu, then "Delete my account and data." Or email us and we'll do it.
  • See or correct what we hold about you: most of it is visible and editable in the app. For anything else, email us.
  • Stop marketing emails with the unsubscribe link in any email, or by emailing us. We'll stop within 10 business days, and usually much sooner. You'll still get sign-in links and messages about purchases you made.
  • Remove yourself from the waitlist by emailing us.

We'll answer privacy requests within 30 days and won't treat you differently for making one. If you live in a state that gives you additional privacy rights, we'll honor them; email us and tell us which state you live in.

8. Security

We protect your information with measures that fit what we hold, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) everywhere,
  • database rules that let each signed-in person reach only their own build,
  • AI output and approvals written only by our server, never directly by the browser,
  • secret keys kept in our hosting provider, never in code,
  • limited access on our side.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you and the authorities as the law requires. Florida law requires notice within 30 days of determining a breach occurred.

9. Information about other people

Your site may mention colleagues, managers, or clients. The interview credits quotes by job title only and drops names, and the privacy step flags client names and colleague names before anything is published. Please only include information about others that you have the right to share.

10. Children

I Made It is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we'll post the new policy with a new effective date and email active customers before it takes effect. We won't use information we already have in a materially different way without your consent.
