Why This Book

Two Ways Companies Get Hurt

Both trace back to the same root cause: no map, just a pile of tactics and a guess about when to use them.

Building too early

A five-person startup with a lead-scoring model and a formal Sales–Marketing SLA before product-market fit isn't ahead of the curve. It's solving a coordination problem that doesn't exist yet.

Building too late

A $30 million company still running pipeline off a personal spreadsheet, with three floating definitions of "qualified lead," isn't being scrappy. It's leaking revenue at a scale that shows up on the board deck.
